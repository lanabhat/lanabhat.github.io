Privacy Policy for YakshaDarshi

Last updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to YakshaDarshi. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you use our application and tell you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

2. Data We Collect

We may collect, use, store and transfer different kinds of personal data about you which we have grouped together as follows:

  • Identity Data includes first name, last name, username or similar identifier, and profile picture (via Google Authentication).
  • Contact Data includes email address.
  • Content Data includes events, images, and descriptions you upload to the platform.

3. How We Use Your Data

We will only use your personal data when the law allows us to. Most commonly, we will use your personal data in the following circumstances:

  • To register you as a new user using Firebase Authentication.
  • To manage your account and content (events and images) hosted on Supabase and Firebase.
  • To display your uploaded community events to other users.

4. Data Security

We have put in place appropriate security measures to prevent your personal data from being accidentally lost, used or accessed in an unauthorised way, altered or disclosed. Your authentication is securely managed by Google Firebase, and images are securely stored via Supabase.

5. Third-Party Links

This application may include links to third-party websites, plug-ins and applications. Clicking on those links or enabling those connections may allow third parties to collect or share data about you. We do not control these third-party websites and are not responsible for their privacy statements.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at yakshagana@gmail.com.
